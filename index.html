Support

Questions and help

Email srinivas@rowdur.com with questions, feedback or problems. It helps to include your phone model and its iOS or Android version. I aim to reply within a few days.

Read the Privacy Policy.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Your fasts, settings and coaching all stay on your phone. There are no accounts, no analytics and no servers. See the Privacy Policy.

Which phones does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later, and Android phones with Android 16 or later. The app works the same on both.

Why isn't the coach sending me messages?

The coach writes its messages with on-device AI. On iPhone that is Apple Intelligence, which needs a supported iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. On Android it is Gemini Intelligence, which needs a supported Android phone. Without it, fasting tracking, live progress and chat still work, but the coach won't send its own nudges.

Also check that notifications are allowed for Fasting Coach. On iPhone: Settings → Notifications → Fasting Coach. On Android: Settings → Apps → Fasting Coach → Notifications. The coach stays quiet during your quiet hours.

What do plans like 16:8 mean?

Hours fasting : hours eating. 16:8 means a 16-hour fast. To change your plan, tap your plan in Profile, or use − and + to set a custom length.

What does Fasting Coach read from my health data?

If you turn it on, it reads steps, active energy, sleep and workouts so the coach can match your day. It only reads this data and never writes to it. On iPhone it reads from Apple Health; on Android it reads from Health Connect.

You can turn this off anytime. On iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Fasting Coach. On Android: switch off Health Connect in Profile, or open Health Connect from Settings → App permissions → Fasting Coach.

Can I use Siri?

On iPhone, yes. Try "Check my fast in Fasting Coach" or "When can I eat in Fasting Coach". You'll find all the shortcuts in the Shortcuts app.

How do I delete my data?

Tap Start over at the bottom of Profile. It erases everything, and so does deleting the app.

Is this medical advice?

No. Fasting Coach offers lifestyle support, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before fasting if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have diabetes or take blood-sugar medication, have a history of eating disorders, take prescription medication, or are under 18. Stop fasting and seek help if you feel unwell.

Privacy policy

We collect nothing.

Effective date: 7 October 2026

This policy covers Fasting Coach for iPhone and for Android. Both apps are designed so that your data never leaves your phone. This policy explains what the app handles and why. The short version: we collect nothing.

What the app stores — on your device only

  • Fasting activity: your goals, fast start/end times, streaks, and history.
  • Coaching data: your preferences (coaching style, quiet hours, message limits), the coach's notes about what kind of support works for you, and a log of the coaching decisions the app made.
  • Conversations: chats with the coach are processed on-device and are not retained as transcripts beyond the active session.

All of this lives in local storage on your phone. It is never uploaded, synced, or shared. Deleting the app deletes it. "Start over" in Profile also erases it.

Health data

Health integration is off until you turn it on. If you enable it, Fasting Coach reads steps, active energy, sleep, and workouts on your device, read-only, solely so coaching can reflect your day:

  • iPhone: read from Apple Health. You can revoke access anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.
  • Android: read from Health Connect. You can revoke access anytime by switching off Health Connect in Profile, or in Health Connect → App permissions → Fasting Coach.

Health data is never written back, never transmitted anywhere, never sold, never used for advertising, and is not used for any purpose other than the coaching shown to you. No person reads it. Fasting Coach's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

On-device AI

Coaching decisions and messages are generated by AI models that run on your phone, and prompts and responses are processed locally:

  • iPhone: Apple Intelligence, using Apple's on-device Foundation Models.
  • Android: Gemini Intelligence, using Google's on-device models.

If a future version offers optional cloud processing on either platform (for example Apple's Private Cloud Compute), it will be off by default, clearly labelled in Profile, used only with your explicit permission, and this policy will be updated first.

Microphone and speech

The voice coach uses the microphone only while you hold a conversation, and speech is transcribed on-device by the phone's built-in speech recognition (Apple's speech frameworks on iPhone, Android's on-device speech recognition on Android). No audio or transcripts leave your phone.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or registration
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting
  • No advertising or tracking of any kind
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No network requests — the app works fully offline

Notifications

Coaching reminders and live fasting progress are local notifications created on your device. You control them through your phone's notification settings (iOS Settings → Notifications, or Android Settings → Apps → Fasting Coach → Notifications) and the app's own quiet hours and message limits.

Deleting your data

Because nothing is stored outside your phone, there is no account or server copy to delete. Tap "Start over" in Profile, or uninstall the app, and all of its data is erased.

Children

Fasting Coach is not directed at children. Fasting is not appropriate for children; consult a doctor for any minor's dietary decisions.

Not medical advice

Fasting Coach offers lifestyle support, not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a qualified professional before making fasting decisions that affect your health, and stop fasting if you feel unwell.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL with a new effective date. Questions: contact srinivas@rowdur.com.
